SCANNING MODES

Three ways to scan.

Companion

Focus directly on a single expiry date for precise scanning and confirmation.

Scout

Search a larger portion of the package automatically when the expiry date is difficult to locate.

Batch

Detect and track multiple visible expiry dates at the same time for faster sorting.

PERFORMANCE

Designed for fast scanning.

LIVE
Camera scanning

ExpiryLens analyzes incoming camera frames continuously while scanning.

~8 ms
Internal processing

Some internal processing stages have completed in as little as approximately 8 milliseconds during testing (using an iPhone from 2021)

MULTI-FRAME
Verification

Multiple frames can be compared before a date is confirmed, helping reduce incorrect detections.

Actual performance varies by device, lighting, camera quality, product movement, and label clarity (it doesn't need to be perfect but it can't be in a dark room or super flashy).

COLOUR CODING

Results at a glance.

GREEN

Not expired

The printed date is today or in the future.

YELLOW

Recently passed

The printed date has passed by less than one year.

RED

One year or more

The printed date is at least one year in the past.

Colour coding is intended as a sorting indicator only and does not determine whether food is safe to consume.

PRIVACY

Built to collect as little as possible.

ExpiryLens does not require an account. Camera access is used for scanning, and the scanner is designed to process images on the device rather than uploading scanned product images to our servers.
